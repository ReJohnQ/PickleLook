About PickleLook

Find pickleball courts, indoor schedules, and nearby public restroom locations.

PickleLook grew out of MorePickleball.org, a community effort to make it easier for everyone to play.

We check court listings using local sources and player tips. See NYC Parks for indoor schedules and NYC Open Data for restroom locations.
