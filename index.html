About PickleLook

Find a court, check indoor schedules, and see where the nearest public restrooms are.

PickleLook grew out of MorePickleball.org, a community effort to make it easier for everyone to play.

Local sources and player tips help us check court listings. Schedules come from NYC Parks and restrooms from NYC Open Data.
